# Morning brief

When to use
- Weekday mornings, before the operator opens the inbox.

Required inputs
- Unread mail
- Today's calendar

Sequence
1. List unread mail. Collapse newsletters to one line.
2. List today's events. Do not confirm holds.
3. Put decisions the human must make at the top.
4. Return a short draft. Do not send mail.

What requires approval
- Sending, deleting, or confirming anything on the calendar.
