# Durable facts

## Language
The operator prefers Korean first, with English when a plugin UI is English-only.

## Morning brief
Unread mail and today's calendar are the only inputs for the morning brief. Newsletters collapse to one line.

## Approval
Outbound mail always waits for a human. Drafts are fine. Sending is not.

## Calendar
Events are never confirmed without asking. A hold may be drafted.

## Privacy
Payroll, contracts, and health details stay out of notes that other bots can read.
